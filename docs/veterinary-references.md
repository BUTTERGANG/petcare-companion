# Veterinary Reference Sources — Industry-Standard Textbooks

The authoritative sources for building our condition KB. Organized by species
and role. These are the textbooks veterinary schools use and clinicians keep
on the shelf.

---

## 🌐 Cross-Species Foundations

| Source | Role | Why It Matters |
|--------|------|----------------|
| **Merck Veterinary Manual** (11th ed, 2016; free online at merckvetmanual.com) | The universal quick reference | Covers ALL domesticated species. First published 1955. Free full-text online — our most accessible primary source. Used by vet students globally. |
| **Plumb's Veterinary Drug Handbook** (10th ed, 2023, Wiley) | Drug formulary gold standard | 738 drug monographs, dosages by species, contraindications. The drug reference every clinic owns. Digital version: plumbs.com |
| **Saunders Comprehensive Veterinary Dictionary** (5th ed) | Terminology | Standard vocabulary source |
| **Veterinary Information Network (VIN.com)** | Practitioner knowledge base | Subscription community + records library; where working vets actually look things up |

## 📚 How We Use These for Our KB

### Structure mapping
Our Condition model fields map directly onto how these texts organize content:
- Ettinger's "problem-oriented approach" = our symptom → condition linkage
- Blackwell's 5-Minute format = our concise entry structure
  (definition/signs/diagnosis/treatment/notes)
- Rebhun's herd-health chapters = our future Herd-level care views
- Merck = freely citable baseline for every entry we write

### Content sourcing strategy
1. **Free tier**: merckvetmanual.com (full text, all species) — cite as baseline
2. **Client-facing language**: Ettinger's 250 client information sheets are the
   exact format precedent for our owner_actions field
3. **Professional depth**: Ettinger/Reed/Rebhun/Pugh for treatment detail
4. **Drug data**: Plumb's monographs if we ever add medication dosing

### Licensing note
We cite these as sources but never reproduce copyrighted text. Facts
(symptoms, dosages, mechanisms) aren't copyrightable — expression is. Our
entries are original writing informed by these references, which is exactly
how the Merck Manual itself operates.
